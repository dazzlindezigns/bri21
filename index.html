        Questionsanswered

        Do I need a special ticket for Drink Around the World?

        No. A standard theme park ticket for EPCOT on Feb 9 is all you need, and you buy drinks as you go. Annual passholders also need a park reservation for that day.

        Can people under 21 come to EPCOT?

        Yes. Anyone with a park ticket can come. You just have to be 21 with a photo ID to buy or drink alcohol. Under-21s walk the same route, hit the food booths, and are in every picture. And no passing drinks to anybody under 21. Disney watches for that.

        Do kids pay?

        Kids under 9 are free for the house and the van. Anyone going into EPCOT still needs a park ticket, and Disney is free for kids under 3.

        When is everything due?

        Is there a payment plan?

        Yes. It's broken into three payments: your house share as the deposit, then your van share in two halves.

        Is $500 the final price?

        Not yet. The $150 house share is set. The van is still an estimate while we shop for the best rate, so that part can move. We'll update this page as soon as it's final. Your deposit is the house share, so you can lock in now.

        What if I pay and then can't make it?

        Can I drive myself instead of riding in the van?

        Talk to Dani first. We're pricing the van by how many people ride, so we need to know before the van gets booked.

        What should I pack?

        Swimsuit, comfortable walking shoes for EPCOT, a pillow and blanket for the ride, your birthday fit, and your photo ID if you're drinking.
